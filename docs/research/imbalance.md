# Imbalance Research

## Research Question

Does a causally defined imbalance feature add structural value beyond the V1 core baseline?

## Hypotheses

No Phase 0 hypothesis claims that imbalance is effective. A future hypothesis must specify the definition, causal availability, and expected incremental result.

## Candidate Methods

Potential definitions may be surveyed in a future research task, but no definition is selected here.

## Data Requirements

Any future test must use chronological XAUUSD data, explicit bar and session semantics, fixed splits, and complete feature provenance.

## No-Lookahead Risks

Definitions that require later filling, mitigation, or surrounding bars must not backdate their availability. Historical visualization cannot substitute for real-time confirmation.

## Metrics

Future work must compare structural metrics such as First Touch Reaction, MFE, MAE, Continued Break Rate, and incremental/ablation lift, including multiple-testing controls.

## Experiment Plan

Only after the V1 baseline is stable: document a falsifiable hypothesis, choose one definition, pre-register parameters, add it as an isolated increment, and ablate it.

## Current Status

**Deferred / Not Core in V1.** No effectiveness is claimed, and no imbalance algorithm is implemented in Phase 0.
