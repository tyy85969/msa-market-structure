# MSA Project Vision

## Identity

MSA means **Multi-Scale Structure Alignment**.

中文名称：**多尺度市场结构共振系统**。

MSA is an independent market-structure research project. Its mission is not to copy, crack, reverse engineer, or reproduce any private indicator. Reference material may motivate questions, but every MSA rule must be derived from general ideas, stated as an explicit hypothesis, and validated with reproducible evidence.

## Mission

Build an explainable and testable framework from these general ideas:

- multi-timeframe market structure;
- historically meaningful price regions;
- explicit structure lifecycle;
- multi-scale alignment and resonance;
- traceable provenance and explainability;
- reproducible validation;
- strict prevention of future-information leakage.

The system must favor what was genuinely knowable in real time over a historically attractive chart.

## Initial research boundary

- Primary research market: **XAUUSD**.
- Current phase: **indicator research**.
- The first objective is stable identification of persistent multi-timeframe upper and lower boundaries with as little structural churn as practical.
- V1 maps structure; it does not generate trading decisions.

MSA is currently **not**:

- an Expert Advisor;
- an automated trading system;
- a high-frequency trading system;
- a buy/sell signal system.

## Evidence standard

Every claimed behavior must be linked to its source type, scale, OriginTime, ConfirmTime, lifecycle state, hypothesis, experiment, and limitations. Screenshots and attractive historical plots are inputs to investigation, not proof.

## Long-term direction

MSA will proceed through a documented baseline, controlled incremental modules, and ablation. Only reviewed modules that improve structural quality without violating the real-time information boundary may advance toward a future Pine implementation.
