# ADR-0003: No Signals in V1

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The V1 objective is to establish reliable and explainable structure. Signal design would mix entry logic, execution assumptions, and performance metrics into an unvalidated structural baseline.

## Decision

V1 does not display buy or sell arrows and does not create automated entries, EA orders, trading strategies, or trading advice.

## Consequences

- Structural quality is evaluated directly rather than through trading outcomes alone.
- The display remains focused on boundaries, lifecycle, and provenance.
- Any future trigger or execution work requires a separate approved phase and evidence.
