# ADR-0001: Primary Market = XAUUSD

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Early validation needs a narrow, stable research boundary. Mixing markets before establishing measurement and lifecycle conventions would add uncontrolled variation.

## Decision

XAUUSD is the primary and first research market for MSA. Initial datasets, hypotheses, examples, and validation reports must identify XAUUSD unless a later approved task explicitly evaluates transferability.

## Consequences

- Research can share one symbol convention and comparable evidence.
- Results are not assumed to generalize to other instruments.
- Multi-market support and transfer studies are deferred.
- This decision does not authorize private data dependencies or trading execution.
