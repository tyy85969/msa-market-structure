# ADR-0006: Primary Objective

## Status

Accepted

## Context

A structure system can overreact to every local fluctuation or remain too slow to describe meaningful boundaries. The first research objective must make that tradeoff explicit.

## Decision

The primary objective is to stably identify multi-timeframe upper and lower boundaries with persistent market meaning while keeping structural churn as low as practical.

The objective is not to catch every small top or bottom.

## Consequences

- Confirmation Delay, False Turn Rate, Trend Capture Ratio, and Box Churn must be considered together.
- A visually exhaustive set of local extrema is not evidence of success.
- Thresholds and tradeoffs require reproducible validation and out-of-sample review.
- Profit metrics alone cannot determine structural quality.
