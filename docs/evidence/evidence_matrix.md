# Evidence Matrix

## Purpose

The matrix links each MSA claim to its evidence class, hypothesis, experiment, and decision. A claim cannot advance because it is repeated across documents or screenshots.

## Evidence classes

1. **Observed** — directly visible or measured in the cited artifact.
2. **High-confidence inference** — strongly suggested by multiple observations but not directly proven.
3. **Low-confidence hypothesis** — plausible and testable, with material uncertainty.
4. **Unknown** — unsupported or currently unresolvable from available material.
5. **Experimental result** — generated by a reproducible, reviewed experiment with declared splits and metrics.

## Update rule

Every new row must identify provenance and limitations. Results derived from Development data cannot be labeled out-of-sample. Unsupported rows remain Unknown or hypothesis-level; they must not be promoted to product claims.
