# Reference Indicator Findings

## Scope and evidence rule

Reference screenshots are used only to formulate independent MSA research questions. They do not establish source code, real-time behavior, causal confirmation, or effectiveness. Every observation and inference below requires later data experiments.

## 1. Observed

These statements describe visible screenshot content only:

- multiple apparent structural boundaries are shown at different visual scales;
- yellow lines appear as a prominent primary-structure layer;
- blue or cyan lines appear as a higher-timeframe reference layer;
- green lines appear as a separately controllable candidate-like layer in some views;
- short lines appear to represent local structure;
- some images show several levels near the same price region;
- ordered screenshots suggest that displayed structure can evolve as the market progresses.

Observed appearance does not establish the rule that generated it.

## 2. High-confidence inference

These interpretations are plausible across multiple visible examples but are not proven:

- the display likely separates primary, higher-timeframe, candidate, and local visual roles;
- some grouped screenshots likely represent consecutive market states, different zoom levels, different timeframes, or module-toggle states;
- the design likely emphasizes multi-scale alignment around persistent upper and lower boundaries.

These remain inferences until reproduced with timestamped evidence.

## 3. Low-confidence hypothesis

These are research hypotheses, not findings:

- a forming structure may progress through a Candidate → Confirmation lifecycle;
- nearby levels from different scales may contribute to an internal resonance concept;
- some lines may freeze after confirmation while candidate lines remain movable;
- color or line length may encode lifecycle, scale, source, or importance.

No private behavior is asserted from these hypotheses.

## 4. Unknown

The screenshots do not establish:

- whether any line repaints or changes after the visible moment;
- the exact real-time confirmation time of any structure;
- the generating algorithm, parameters, lifecycle rules, or source code;
- whether visible arrows or text were produced by the indicator or added manually;
- the exact meaning of labels 30 / 1 / 2 / 4 / 12 / 24 / 日 / 周;
- whether nearby lines are truly independent evidence;
- how data gaps, sessions, symbols, or timeframe completion are handled.

## Mandatory cautions

- Static screenshots cannot prove that an indicator does not repaint.
- Static screenshots cannot prove the real-time confirmation moment.
- Reference screenshots may contain manual arrows and text annotations.
- Manual annotations must not be mistaken for indicator output.
- The precise semantics of 30 / 1 / 2 / 4 / 12 / 24 / 日 / 周 have not been proven.
- MSA does not claim to have restored, decoded, or reproduced any private indicator source code.

## Independent MSA direction

MSA may independently investigate multi-timeframe structural boundaries, confirmed primary structure, higher-timeframe references, optional forming candidates, local structure, multi-scale alignment, and explicit lifecycle behavior. MSA colors are governed by ADR-0005 and are not claims about the reference system.

**All content in this document requires subsequent validation through chronological data experiments.**
